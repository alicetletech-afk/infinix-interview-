05 MANAGEMENTHow do you manage marketing? +

I normally start from the business objective, then work down into strategy, budget, communication, resources and execution.

I stay hands-on where it makes sense, but I also look at what I should own, what the team can handle and when we need a specialist.

KEY: Budget = strategic resource, not just a spending limit.
06 LIVE CASEHow would you launch a new Infinix product? +

Let me structure my thinking first.

First, I’d understand the product, the target consumer and what we want to achieve.

Then I’d find the strongest reason for the consumer to care and build the campaign around that.

After that, I’d think about how to make them notice the product, believe the message, experience it and eventually consider buying it — then choose the right channels for each stage.

KEY: What are we selling? · Who are we selling to? · Why would they care? → Notice → Believe → Experience → Buy
07 PRODUCT IDEAGive me an actual product launch idea. +

I’d build the idea around the product’s strongest reason to buy, rather than starting from a channel.

If gaming is the hero, I’d work with the gaming ecosystem — esports teams, players, tournaments, creators or communities — and let people actually use the device.

If the hero is camera, design, value or AI, I’d change the idea around that strength and build the consumer experience from there.

KEY: Strongest product benefit → Real experience → Content / PR / Community / Conversion
08 GAMING LAUNCHGive me a gaming launch idea for Infinix. +

I’d rather make the phone part of the gaming experience than only advertise the specs.

For example, partner with an esports team or tournament and have professional players actually use the device. That creates real product proof.

Then extend the partnership into player content, PR, fan activities and product trials.

KEY: Actual player use → Product proof → Content → PR → Fan experience
09 COLLAB CASEHow would you turn a partnership into an activation? +

I’d start with what can we give them, and what can they give us?

Then I’d find a role for the product inside the experience — not just logo exposure.

From that one partnership, I’d try to create product proof, content, PR, community engagement and a path to conversion.

KEY: Give ↔ Get → Product role → Amplify → Conversion
10 GAMINGWhat if gaming is the key selling point? +

I’d look at the gaming ecosystem, not only product advertising.

We could work with esports teams, players, tournaments or gaming communities. If professional players actually use the device, the product itself becomes proof.

Then we can extend that into content, PR and community engagement.

KEY: Real usage → Product proof → Content / PR / Community

My advantage isn’t only the language. I’m used to working across different cultures, and I can communicate directly with Chinese and international teams.

With HQ, I first understand the objective and why they want something. Then I think about how to make it work in Thailand, with less communication gap between HQ direction and local execution.

Localization is not just translation. We keep the core objective, but adapt the execution.
